Leinore, Coven Counters

A Selesnya +1/+1 counters deck, modified from a Midnight Hunt Commander precon. At the start of each of your combats, Leinore puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature you control, then draws you a card if you control three or more creatures with different powers. Counters are how this deck makes powers different, so most of the 99 either place counters, grow themselves or pay off creatures that carry them. It runs 38 creatures, 24 of them Humans, and a second layer of Human payoffs. This page is the starting point for the evaluation: what the deck does today, by effect.

Starting the evaluation

What the checks flagged

  • Colour sources are short for the double pips22 white sources where Knight of the White Orchid's {W}{W} wants 30, and 23 green where Kurbis's {G}{G} wants 30. 12 Forests and 12 Plains split the basics evenly.
  • Card advantage is below target9 cards against 12-14: a 67% chance to see one by turn 4, against a 75% target. Leinore's own draw comes on top, but only once three creatures have different powers.
  • Interaction is thin5 targeted answers against 7-9, no edicts and no one-sided wipes. Most of the removal is artifact and enchantment removal (Return to Dust, Ruinous Intrusion).
  • Wipes against your own boardCleansing Nova destroys every creature, Leinore included. Celestial Judgment lets you keep one creature per power, so you can keep her. Deckwright's review also flags Angel of Glory's Rise as a -X/-X sweeper; it only exiles Zombies, so that warning is a misread.
  • One wipe takes the mana too8 mana sources are creatures, so a creature wipe takes the ramp and the threats together. 13 of 37 lands are nonbasic, enough for Blood Moon effects to bite.
  • Cards the strength model cannot value yetCurse of Conformity, Dearly Departed, Omnipresence, Sigarda's Vanguard, Ugin's Conjurant and Wall of Mourning read as "does no job the model values". That is a gap in the model (open issues OI-29 to OI-45), not a verdict on the cards.
