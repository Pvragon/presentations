Game plan

Three layers, each enough on its own

The horde

Go wide early

Fast mana into token makers: Cryptbreaker, Diregraf Colossus, Endless Ranks of the Dead, Tormod, the Desecrator and Dread Summons. Necroduality copies every nontoken Zombie that enters.

The drain

Every body pays twice

Scarab God drains each opponent for every Zombie in your upkeep. Bastion of Remembrance, Funeral Room, Plague Belcher and Ninja Teen drain on deaths. Grave Pact and Dictate of Erebos turn each death into an edict. Skullclamp, Dark Prophecy and Undead Augur draw off the same deaths.

The backup

Combos you draw into

Loop pieces, sacrifice outlets and payoffs that swap for one another. No tutor finds them.

Scarab God and opponents' graveyards

For {2}{U}{B}, Scarab God exiles a creature card from any graveyard and makes a 4/4 Zombie copy. Aimed at opponents' graveyards, it steals their best creatures and removes their recursion at the same time. Grave Pact, Dictate of Erebos and Altar of Dementia keep those graveyards stocked. Animate Dead and Reanimate take from them too. Unlike your current deck, this one runs nothing that exiles a graveyard.
