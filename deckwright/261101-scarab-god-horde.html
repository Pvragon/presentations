Game plan

Three layers, each enough on its own

The horde

Go wide early

Fast mana into token makers: Cryptbreaker, Diregraf Colossus, Endless Ranks of the Dead, Teval's Judgment, Necroduality and Reflections of Littjara copying every Zombie, Army of the Damned at the top.

The drain

Every body pays twice

Scarab God drains per Zombie each upkeep. Vengeful Dead, Bastion of Remembrance, Funeral Room, The Meathook Massacre and Plague Belcher drain on every death, and Grave Pact turns each one into an edict. Skullclamp and Liliana, Dreadhorde General draw off the same deaths.

The backup

Combos you draw into

Loop pieces, sacrifice outlets and payoffs that swap for one another. No tutor finds them.

Scarab God and opponents' graveyards

For {2}{U}{B}, Scarab God exiles a creature card from any graveyard and makes a 4/4 Zombie copy. Aimed at opponents' graveyards, it steals their best creatures and removes their recursion at the same time. Altar of Dementia and Grave Pact keep their graveyards stocked. Bojuka Bog, which would empty a graveyard Scarab God wants, is out.

Update 2026-11-01

The package pass

Every package was compared against every legal blue-black card that does its job (house rules applied), with newer cards considered on merit. Scarab God now aims at opponents' graveyards, which doubles as graveyard hate, so Bojuka Bog is out. Tapped lands were swapped for untapped ones where the budget allowed. The full package lists are on the Packages page: https://prez.prgn.ai/deckwright/261101-scarab-god-packages

In 7

  • The Theorist, Jace Beleren
  • 2026. Draws a card in each opponent's draw step: three extra cards a round at a four-player table.
  • Fountainport
  • 2024, owned. A utility land that turns spare tokens into cards, Fish or Treasure.
  • Funeral Room // Awakening Hall
  • 2024. Each creature you control that dies drains each opponent; the second door returns creatures later.
  • Swiftfoot Boots
  • Owned. Hexproof and haste for Scarab God; protection was down to one or two cards.
  • Teval's Judgment
  • 2025, owned. A card, Treasure or Zombie whenever cards leave your graveyard, which Gisa and Geralf and the loops do every turn.
  • The Soul Stone
  • 2025, owned. A 2-mana black rock, indestructible; late in the game it returns a creature from your graveyard every upkeep.
  • Skullclamp
  • Draws two whenever the equipped creature dies, for any reason. With free sacrifice outlets it is a draw engine.

Combo web
