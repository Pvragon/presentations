Commander deck · blue-black · bracket 4 · token combat · v9 · 2026-11-01

The Scarab God Onslaught

The same Zombie horde, built to win in the red zone. It floods the board, stacks anthems, makes the army hard to block, and swings. Scarab God steals from opponents' graveyards between attacks.

Card data: Scryfall, updated 2026-10-30 · combos: Commander Spellbook, 2026-10-24 · rules verified 2026-10-20 · power: edhpowerlevel.com, run 2026-11-01 · prices: Scryfall cheapest paper non-foil · card images: Scryfall

Game plan

Go wide, get bigger, get through

Width

17 token makers

Army of the Damned, Josu Vess kicked, Endless Ranks of the Dead, Liliana's Mastery, Necroduality and Reflections of Littjara, and Field of the Dead on land drops.

Size

10 anthems

Death Baron, Lord of the Accursed, Cemetery Reaper, Diregraf Captain, Undead Warchief, Mikaeus, Liliana's Mastery, and Coat of Arms, Door of Destinies and Vanquisher's Banner scaling with the board.

Reach

8 ways past blockers

Cover of Darkness (fear), Zombie Master with Urborg (swampwalk against everyone), Hordewing Skaab and Eternal Skylord (flying), Gleaming Overseer (hexproof and menace for tokens), Lost Monarch of Ifnir (afflict) and Akroma's Memorial (haste).

Clearing the way

Crippling Fear wipes non-Zombie blockers and spares your board. Grave Pact turns trades into edicts. Cyclonic Rift on overload clears the table the turn you swing.

The attack payoffs

Archnemesis drains 2 and draws a card on every attack against its player. Throne of the God-Pharaoh drains each opponent for every tapped attacker at end of turn. Riverchurn Monument keeps opponents' graveyards full for Scarab God.

Update 2026-11-01

The package pass

Every package was compared against every legal blue-black card that does its job (house rules applied), with newer cards considered on merit. Scarab God now aims at opponents' graveyards, which doubles as graveyard hate, so Bojuka Bog is out. Tapped lands were swapped for untapped ones where the budget allowed. The full package lists are on the Packages page: https://prez.prgn.ai/deckwright/261101-scarab-god-packages

  • 2025. Mills opponents each turn, so Scarab God always has targets in their graveyards.
