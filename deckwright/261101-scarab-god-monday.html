The card pool

What "owned" means here

Owned means: in the 2026-10-22 Moxfield collection export, in the current Moxfield deck or sideboard, or named by you (Fierce Guardianship, Akroma's Memorial). No ordered cards have been named yet.

Bracket 3, not 4

Three Game Changers (Grim Monolith, Fierce Guardianship, Cyclonic Rift) and no two-card combos, so this list is legal at a bracket 3 table too.

One card to sequence

Kindred Dominance naming Zombie destroys Scarab God (a God) and Gisa and Geralf; Scarab God returns to your hand at the next end step. Use your commander's value first.

Update 2026-11-01

The package pass

Every package was compared against every legal card, and every owned card that fits now competes for a slot. Five owned 2024-2025 cards came in. Tapped lands were replaced with untapped owned lands and basics. Package lists: https://prez.prgn.ai/deckwright/261101-scarab-god-packages

In 16

  • Dimir Signet
  • A plain 2-mana rock in place of Basalt Monolith.
  • Cursecloth Wrappings
  • 2025, owned. A Zombie anthem that also embalms creature cards from your graveyard.
  • Swiftfoot Boots
  • Owned. Hexproof and haste for Scarab God; protection was down to one or two cards.
  • Teval's Judgment
  • 2025, owned. A card, Treasure or Zombie whenever cards leave your graveyard, which Gisa and Geralf and the loops do every turn.
  • Island×2
  • A basic in place of a tapped land.
  • Exotic Orchard
  • Untapped land in place of a tapped one.
  • Swamp×2
  • A basic in place of a tapped land.
  • Unclaimed Territory
  • Untapped land for Zombie spells, in place of a tapped one.
  • Spire of Industry
  • Untapped land, any colour while you control an artifact.
  • Fountainport
  • 2024, owned. A utility land that turns spare tokens into cards, Fish or Treasure.
  • Thran Dynamo
  • Owned. Three colourless every turn from turn 4; untaps normally.
  • The Soul Stone
  • 2025, owned. A 2-mana black rock, indestructible; late in the game it returns a creature from your graveyard every upkeep.
  • Skullclamp
  • Draws two whenever the equipped creature dies, for any reason. With free sacrifice outlets it is a draw engine.
  • Will of the Abzan
  • 2025, owned. With a commander out, every opponent sacrifices their biggest creature and loses 3, and you return creatures from your graveyard.
