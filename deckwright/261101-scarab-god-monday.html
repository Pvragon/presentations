The Scarab God, Monday List

The strongest Scarab God deck you can sleeve from your collection today. A Zombie horde that goes wide, attacks through the air and past blockers, and drains the table each time a Zombie dies. Gisa and Geralf recast Zombies from the graveyard, and Scarab God steals from opponents' graveyards. It keeps the Zombie core of your current Gisa and Geralf deck and drops its self-mill and graveyard-casting cards. The architecture comes first: 29 creature cards, 33 sources of bodies, and interaction split by kind for a four-player table.

Play notes

Things to know at the table

Crippling Fear is one-sided

Name Zombie. Your creatures that are not Zombies are Scarab God (5/5), Gisa and Geralf (4/4) and Ghoulcaller Gisa (3/4), and all three survive -3/-3. Deckwright's review warns that it kills Scarab God; it does not.

Ghoulcaller Gisa turns one body into several

Pay {B}, sacrifice a creature, and get Zombie tokens equal to its power. With lords out, a token is 3/3 or bigger, so one sacrifice nets two or more bodies and triggers every death drain.

Two combo lines, both three cards

Rooftop Storm and Ashnod's Altar loop with Gravecrawler or with Liliana, Untouched by Death. Bastion of Remembrance or Vengeful Dead turns either loop into a win. Neither is a two-card combo, so bracket 3 holds.
