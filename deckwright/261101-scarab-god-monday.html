The Scarab God, Monday List

The strongest Scarab God deck you can sleeve from your collection today. It has the Horde's shape: wide board, death drains, Gisa and Geralf recasting Zombies, and Scarab God stealing from opponents' graveyards. It keeps the Zombie core of your current Gisa and Geralf deck and drops its self-mill and graveyard-casting cards.

The card pool

What "owned" means here

Owned means: in the 2026-10-22 Moxfield collection export, in the current Moxfield deck or sideboard, or named by you, such as Fierce Guardianship. No ordered cards have been named yet.

Bracket 3, not 4

Three Game Changers (Grim Monolith, Fierce Guardianship, Cyclonic Rift) and no two-card combos, so this list is legal at a bracket 3 table too.

One card to sequence

Kindred Dominance naming Zombie destroys Scarab God (a God) and Gisa and Geralf; Scarab God returns to your hand at the next end step. Use your commander's value first.

Two combo lines, both three cards

Rooftop Storm and Ashnod's Altar loop with Gravecrawler or with Liliana, Untouched by Death. Bastion of Remembrance or Vengeful Dead turns either loop into a win. Neither is a two-card combo, so bracket 3 holds.
