What the simulation does not model

  • colours are not modelled: a card is castable when the mana total allows it, so colour screw is invisible
  • the commander is a free spell of its mana value in the opening hand (no tax, no recasting)
  • mana creatures cost one mana; two-mana dorks and rocks above two mana count as ordinary curve cards
  • land-fetch ramp is modelled for basic-land fetches that cost two or three mana
  • each draw and fetch effect is rounded to one shared setting per type (see notes)
  • interaction seen by turn 4 counts the first 7 + 4 cards of the kept shuffle (cards bottomed on a mulligan included)
  • Astral Cornucopia (mana value 0) counted as a 1-drop
  • Everflowing Chalice (mana value 0) counted as a 1-drop
  • 6 draw engines share one setting: mana value 3
