Deck cards outside the packages

Every card in the six Zombie decks, checked against the packages. 45 fall outside them.

  • 17 are left out on purpose, mostly tapped lands and fetch lands, which a two-colour deck doesn't need.
  • 17 do a job no package covers yet. Most belong to Gisa and Geralf's graveyard plan: self-mill, casting from the graveyard and mass reanimation. Tutors and card selection are missing too. These are the next packages to build.
  • 7 are good cards the packages missed: Cleaver Skaab, Drown in the Loch, Open the Graves, Scourge of Nel Toth, Spark Reaper, Vampiric Rites, Vindictive Lich. They're in now.
  • 4 never reached a candidate pool, because Deckwright has no tag for what they do: Champion of the Perished, Mire Triton, Throne of the God-Pharaoh, Unbreathing Horde.
