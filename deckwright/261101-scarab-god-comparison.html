3 · Which to play

My recommendation

  • Monday: the Monday ListIt is the only one you can sleeve without buying anything, and at 7.0 it is already well above your current deck's 5.89. It is also legal at a bracket 3 table.
  • First upgrade: the Horde budget build$379 for power 8.11, the most power per dollar here. It keeps all 19 of the Horde's combo lines.
  • If your table is creature-heavy and slow: the OnslaughtCrippling Fear, Graf Harvest and Zombie Master punish boards of blockers. Its budget version costs $398 for power 7.66.
  • Full powerThe Horde at $2,036, over half of it one card, Underground Sea. Its budget version rates 8.11 for $379.
