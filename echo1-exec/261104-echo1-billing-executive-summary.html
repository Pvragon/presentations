Every gap between billable and paid has a named fix

Each step says what is in it and what clearing it takes, in the same order as the chart.

  1. Contract invoices

    Rides for contract customers. They are billed on monthly invoices, which this page does not track, and never become claims.

    Fix: Confirm each customer's invoice went out and was paid.

  2. Not classified

    Rides not yet tagged as contract or insurance work, so nothing has been billed.

    Fix: Tag each ride as contract or insurance so it can be billed.

  3. Insurance, no claim

    Insurance rides with no claim yet. Most have no payer on file.

    Fix: Find each rider's payer, then add the rides to the next claim build.

  4. 30-mile under-billing

    Claims for rides over 30 miles, billed below the contract schedule.

    Fix: Correct the rate on these claims and resubmit them.

  5. 30-mile over-billing

    Claims for rides of 30 miles or less, billed per mile above the Medicaid flat fee before Aug 18.

    Fix: Correct these claims down before the payer recoups the difference.

  6. Other pricing gaps

    Small differences between a claim's charge and the ride's contract price.

    Fix: Check the largest few for a wrong rate.

  7. Waiting on payer

    Claims sent and not yet paid.

    Fix: Follow up on claims older than 30 days.

  8. Denied or rejected

    Claims the payer turned down or sent back.

    Fix: Fix the reason given and resubmit.

  9. Short-paid

    Claims paid for less than the charge.

    Fix: Appeal the shortfall with the payer.

  10. Other payment gaps

    Leftover differences between charge and payment on paid claims.

    Fix: No action needed at its current size.

481 insurance rides worth $174k have no claim yet. The largest group has no payer on file because coverage was not found or is inactive. Another 107 rides go out with the next claim build.

Correcting long-ride claims is worth $312k. Checking contract invoices covers $220k, and billing the open insurance rides at the correct rate adds $174k. Separately, $11k billed on short-ride claims may have to be repaid.

Rides in two actions are counted once in the total: 296 rides, 29,825 miles.

How the numbers are made

Figures come from the billing model run on 2026-11-03 20:09 PT, for rides in Aug 1 - Nov 3 2026. Ride dollars use the contract price. Claim dollars come from the claim.

  • Billable means a ride that was not cancelled and not marked Non-Billable.
  • Claimed means a billable ride on a live claim. Merged and voided claims do not count.
  • Received means money has been paid on the ride's live claim.
  • Processing is the ops step that checks each ride before it is billed.
  • Months are months of service. August is partial: Echo1 carried few rides before Aug 11. November covers only its first three days.
  • Each ride counts once. Miles are loaded miles, pickup to dropoff. Test rides are left out.
  • Flat monthly contract fees count for August to October. November's are left out.
  • Rides for Integris and Choctaw, whose contracts have ended, and for Nebraska, which has no payer yet, count nothing.
