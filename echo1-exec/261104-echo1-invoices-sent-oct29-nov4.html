How this is counted

  • Week = one billing week, Thursday to Wednesday. This week is the 5 invoices sent since last Thursday; the previous week is the 5 invoices before them.
  • Invoice = one claim batch (RC-B-####), the same rows as Billing → Accounting with the echo1 switch on. "Sent" is when the batch went to Waystar, in Central time.
  • Billed = the claims' total charges. A claim belongs to the batch it first went out in.
  • Received = payments recorded on those claims (Waystar remittances and payments attached by hand), the Accounting tab's own sum. Claims paid = claims with any money in. No take-backs on any of these invoices.
  • Rework = claims denied, rejected, reversed or partially paid, or with a line cut under a denial code. Rework ratio = rework claims ÷ claims the payer has answered (paid, partially paid or denied), and ÷ all claims billed. Codes and amounts come from the EOB lines stored on each claim; what each code means is the payer's own glossary wording.
  • Still owed = each claim's balance, never below zero (the Dashboard's Total Remaining rule).
  • Payer = the payer the claim was billed to. The payer and status splits were checked to add back up to each invoice's Accounting-tab total, to the cent.
