What each item is

ItemOwnerDescription
Crisis Rebuild & Manager Dispatch ApprovalJP + PabloCrisis-versus-scheduled classification carried from intake to billing, plus manager approval before dispatch where required.
Driver Void RequestsVictorDrivers ask dispatch to void a single ride before the route starts, with a short window to take it back.
Payer Table ReworkRomanPayer table rebuilt to carry detailed contract terms per payer, with payer identities consolidated.
V1 ShutdownRafRemaining reports and data moved off the old platform, and the old platform decommissioned.
Reporting Tools (Ops Report, Dispatcher Productivity)Alex (PT)The Operations Report and the Dispatcher Productivity report rebuilt on Echo1 data.
Driver Vehicle Assignment FixJP + PabloEcho1 becomes the source of truth for driver-vehicle assignment, per trip, with one-way sync to Samsara and dispatcher-only changes.
Driver Change Stop OrderVictorDrivers can work an optimized route's stops in a different order when the day requires it.
Finance Dashboard & AnalyticsRomanBilling-team views of billed, paid, outstanding and denied revenue by payer and period, with export.
Audit Log & Transport EventsRafEvery change recorded automatically, and a complete event history for each transport.
Tools AreaAlex (PT)One Tools area holding the dispatch wall board, the lobby ops dashboard, data export and audit documents.
Improved Client Risk Flags and Transport NotesVictorClient safety flags and ride notes visible to drivers and dispatchers, editable after dispatch, with the operational ban kept separate.
Audit Docs Download ToolAlex (PT)One-click download of a ride's or claim's document package: trip log PDF, signatures, history.
Transport Dashboard Unification (a.k.a. situational awareness + gap finder)JP + PabloOne live command center combining the transport dashboard, the situational awareness map and Huy's schedule gap finder.
Intake Form & Configuration FinalizationVictorTools for RideCare to configure and create its own intake forms and fields, with training material.
Contract Billing WorkflowRomanInvoices generated from completed rides at contract rates for customers not billed through insurance claims.
Test Data LinterRafA nightly check that finds QA test records in production and clears them out.
Finalize Tools & Reporting AreasAlex (PT)The Tools and Reports areas finished and handed over to RideCare for daily use.
Missed Request ReworkJP + PabloMissed requests go to an active review queue instead of sitting in a dead-end status.
Client Email RepliesVictorReplies to Echo1's emails reach the right inbox and thread into the right conversation.
AI Support ChatJP + PabloAn in-app AI assistant that answers dispatcher and driver questions, opened up beyond the admin pilot.
NIST 800-53 & CFR 42 ComplianceRafSecurity controls and evidence aligned with NIST 800-53, plus 42 CFR Part 2 confidentiality for substance-use rides.
In App Chat + Issue LogAlex (PT)Messaging between drivers, dispatchers and managers inside Echo1, plus an issue log tied to each transport.
Manual Dispatch UnificationJP + PabloSingle and bulk dispatch both run through the unified route drawer, so there is one way to dispatch.
Asset/Vehicle Mgmt OverhaulVictorVehicles and assets managed in one place in Echo1: onboarding, maintenance booking and Samsara alerts.
APIRafA read-only API giving internal staff and approved systems controlled, auditable access to Echo1 data, for compliance.
Relay Trip Dispatch/HandlingVictorDispatching rides handed from one driver to another mid-route, with every leg tracked.
Relay Trip BillingRomanRelay trips billed correctly, leg by leg, under each payer's contract terms.
Release Materials & TrainingRomanRelease notes and training material for RideCare's billing team on the new billing tools.
Driver Schedule MgmtVictorDriver availability and shift schedules managed in Echo1.
Omnipresent Help IconsJP + PabloA help icon on every core page that opens the matching help article.
V2 Billing FinalizationRomanRemaining billing work closed out so all billing runs in Echo1.
Client Cancellations + QueueJP + PabloClient cancellation calls handled in a call-center queue inside Echo1, rebuilt from Huy's tool.
Multi Factor AuthenticationRafTwo-step sign-in required for admins and dispatchers.
Client Belongings CaptureVictorDrivers record a client's belongings at pickup and confirm the handoff at drop-off.
Database integrity monitoringRafAutomated checks that flag broken or inconsistent records before they affect operations or billing.
Offline Driver AccessJP + PabloDrivers keep their route and record their work without signal; it syncs when they reconnect.
Route Deviation AlertsJP + PabloAdmins are alerted when a driver strays from the planned route.
Feature Release WalkthroughsJP + PabloGuided in-app tours that walk users through each new feature when it ships.
Branded Client Login PagesVictorEach requesting agency gets its own branded login page.
Trip Log & Processing AlertsJP + PabloAlerts for incomplete trip logs and for rides stuck before processing.
Overdue trip log enforcementJP + PabloWarnings, then lockout with an admin override, for trip logs left unsigned past their deadline.
