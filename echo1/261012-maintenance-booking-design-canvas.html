Driver maintenance booking, and crash alerts

An approved appointment reserves the vehicle, and a Samsara crash alert pulls it off the road. Either way, only the fleet manager puts it back in service.

Mental modelProcess flowScreensDecisions and notesImpact

Mental model

Today, booking maintenance happens in Teams and email to Bison Auto, and Echo1 never hears about it. Crashes reach leadership by phone. In both cases dispatch can keep assigning a vehicle that isn't on the road.

Why users need this
Service is dueSamsara tells the driver. The driver messages someone at Bison and may never hear back with a time.
Booked, but dispatchedDispatch has no record of the appointment, so trips still land on the vehicle while it's in the shop.
Crash on the roadLeadership finds out and phones the driver. The vehicle still shows In Service in Echo1.
The one action
“Request maintenance”

On the driver's reminder banner and in Forms. It opens one drawer that holds the whole request: what's due, open times at Bison, the status, and later Car returned. The driver never changes the vehicle's status; Echo1 and the fleet manager do.

Pending→ New time offered→ Approved→ In shop→ Returned→ Completed orDeniedCancelled
Vehicle: In Service⇄In Maintenance
Tempting: give the vehicle back automaticallyPut it back In Service when the driver taps “Car returned” or when the window ends. A vehicle still on the lift, or one that failed its repair, goes straight back to dispatch, and nobody confirms the work was done.
Chosen: the fleet manager closes every appointment“Car returned” only tells the fleet manager. The vehicle stays In Maintenance until they confirm. An appointment still open after its window is flagged as overdue on the Fleet page, never closed by itself. Crash alerts follow the same rule.
Guardrails: Drivers act only on their own vehicle Approve, deny, close and slots need fleet:write Deny needs a reason Only dispatch blocks the driver A failed notification never blocks a status change

Process flow

Two flows. Booking runs from Samsara's due notice to the fleet manager's sign-off. A crash skips the request entirely: the alert takes the vehicle off the road first.

Decisions and notes

Settled with Tom Drennan (RideCARE) and Victor on Oct 9–12. Full reasoning and sources are in the #530 proposal.

Decided
Decided — off dispatchThe vehicle, by default. Dispatch can block the driver too for a given appointment.
Decided — who approvesThe fleet manager (Mitchell Temple, manager role) or management (Misty Messenger, Johnny Burgos). fleet:write is enough; no new role.
Decided — completionMechanics aren't on Echo1. The driver taps Car returned; the fleet manager confirms. Overdue appointments are flagged on the Fleet page and never close by themselves.
Decided — capacityEcho1 doesn't track slot capacity. The fleet manager decides when a request comes in.
Decided — where drivers goNo new tab. A drawer from the reminder banner or Forms holds the whole request and its status.
Decided — bannerDrivers can hide the reminder. It comes back until they book or the item is dismissed.
Decided — slotsIn scope (P3). The fleet manager enters Bison and RideCARE's open slots by hand until a calendar exists to read from.
Decided — source of truthSamsara holds service schedules. Due notices go to anyone who drives that vehicle, plus the fleet manager and management.
Decided — crashesA Samsara crash alert puts the vehicle In Maintenance at once and alerts the fleet manager and leadership. Only the fleet manager puts it back. No RingCentral work now (#536 is the later path).
Engineering
Engineering — Samsara webhookEcho1 is subscribed only to four route-stop events. AlertIncident is on the allowed list but not subscribed, and classifyEvent acknowledges unknown events without acting. Crash and maintenance alerts both need a subscription and a handler.
Engineering — maintenance sourceSamsara's schedules API is beta and returns intervals, not due dates. The plan is the Scheduled Maintenance alert to the webhook. Check PredictiveMaintenanceAlert and WorkOrderCreatedOrChanged first. Fallback: intervals against last_odometer_miles.
Engineering — crash backupSamsara's safety events API lists Crash events. Poll every few minutes, dedupe on the Samsara id. Needs the “Read Safety Events & Scores” scope.
Engineering — new tablesvehicle_maintenance_due, maintenance_appointment, maintenance_slot, vehicle_note, vehicle_status_change. One migration PR under the post-baseline gate; the last two are shared by both tickets.
Engineering — dispatchIn Maintenance is already skipped by dispatch and the optimizer. New: the picker reads reservations before the window (frame 15), and a time-boxed driver block.
Engineering — UI that's newNo calendar or week view exists (frame 14). Driver screens use bottom dialogs, not drawers. Fleet gets a fifth tab. The project-status note “Fleet admin is not driver shell” needs updating.
Engineering — notificationsNew template keys for due, requested, approved, new time, denied, reminder, returned, overdue and crash. A maintenanceRecipients list modeled on crashReportRecipients. Driver SMS respects opt-in.
Open
Open — tows · RideCARE's Samsara adminSamsara documents no tow alert. What flags a tow today? Answer expected Oct 13. Until then, drivers report tows with Alert dispatch (frame 18).
Open — reminders (Q7) · Macie, MistyWhen reminders go out, and whether SMS or email is allowed for due notices and appointment reminders.
Open — defect reports (Q9) · Macie, MistyDoes the existing Maintenance Request feed this flow, or stay a separate defect report (frame 10)?
Open — Samsara setup · RomanCreating the Scheduled Maintenance and crash alerts and pointing them at Echo1's webhook is Samsara-side work.
Open — naming · Victor“Book maintenance” beside “Maintenance Request” in Forms may confuse drivers. Rename the old one to “Report a vehicle issue”?

Impact

What this fixes
  • Drivers see due maintenance in Echo1 without opening Samsara (Tom's minimum).
  • Booking moves out of Teams and email, with a status the driver can check.
  • Dispatch can't assign a vehicle that's booked into the shop (William's rule).
  • Nothing returns to service without the fleet manager confirming it.
  • Crashes reach the fleet manager and leadership in seconds, with the vehicle already off the road.
  • Every vehicle status change records who, when and why.
What it still needs
  • Answers on tows (Q12), reminders (Q7) and defect reports (Q9).
  • Roman to set up the Samsara alerts; test one maintenance and one crash alert end to end before P1 and the crash work ship.
  • The migration in its own PR, coordinated with deploys.
  • Someone to keep slots current; stale slots mean drivers pick times Bison can't take.
  • Rough size: P0 ~1 day, P1 4–5, P2 8–10, P3 5–7, crash and tow 4–6.
