RideCare · Payer Rate Encoding — Proposal

transport → payer → rate_schedule → rate_line

A proposed encoding for payer rate schedules. A transport resolves its payer; the payer points at the rate_schedule it currently prices against; the schedule owns its rate_line rows as a real one-to-many. This proposal covers payer rate encoding only — it does not change 837 filing.

transport N ──1 payer N ──1 rate_schedule 1 ──< ∞ rate_line

01The model

A transport resolves its payer; the payer always points at the current rate_schedule; the schedule owns its rate_line rows.

⤢click to zoom

Rate history is preserved by stamping the resolved rate_schedule_id onto the record at pricing time, so a past ride never reprices at today's rate.

03Versioning

  • A rate change is an INSERT of a new rate_schedule row (+ its rate_line rows) — never edit one that's already been billed against.
  • Same schedule_key, incremented version — a genuinely different schedule gets a new schedule_key.
  • payer.rate_schedule_id repoints to the new version; old rides are unaffected.
  • The resolved rate_schedule_id is stamped on the record at pricing time, never recomputed — that's what preserves history.

04Three billable grains

rate_schedule.billable_grain decides what the biller iterates — independent of the rate math.

grainmeaning
person_legone client A→B
vehicle_tripone vehicle trip, 1..N patients, ONE unit — billed per trip, not per person (e.g. Sedgwick: "per trip, not per person charge (one or more patients)")
frequencyone payer × one period, fixed charge
