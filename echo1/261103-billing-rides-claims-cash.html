3. Why monthly revenue is below the spring

Ride volume

PrescheduledCrisis

Prescheduled rides from about 15 referring programs stopped in May and June, and billable rides fell by about 6,000 a month. Those rides were billed to SoonerCare and the MCOs. Claimed value fell from between $2.1M and $2.4M a month to between $0.56M and $0.71M.

The Aug 18 rate change

From Aug 18 the contract includes the first 30 loaded miles in the $160 base, so a ride under 30 miles is $160 flat. Insurance ride counts stayed at about 1,900 a month, but the change took about $87k off August (from the 18th), $194k off September and $184k off October: $464k so far, 23% of a full month’s value. That is the contract, so it isn’t recoverable.

Cash received

HumanaAll other payers

January includes a one-off $655k Humana overpayment on a single ride. Humana took back $310k in April and May by withholding whole payments, and about $345k is still to come off. Contract payers pay outside Waystar and aren’t included.

What each number means
  • Billable value prices each ride by the payer’s contract on the ride date: $160 a trip plus $4.80 a loaded mile (pickup to drop-off). From Aug 18, 2026 the first 30 loaded miles are included in the $160. Contract payers use their own rates, and rides with no payer use SoonerCare’s. These values are estimates.
  • Claimed is the total on claims for that month’s rides, from echo1 or V1. Received is what payers have paid on those claims so far.
  • Claim totals land within about 1% of the estimate each month.
  • Months are the day of the ride, Oklahoma time. Cash received is by payment date.
  • Contract work is rides for contract payers and prescheduled rides with no payer. Rides outside Oklahoma are shown on their own.
  • Insurance rides are the ones that should become SoonerCare, DMH or MCO claims.
  • echo1 rides are transport numbers 300000 and up, without 18 QA test rides. Claim counts leave out the V1 migration backfill.
  • Mileage billing: before Dec 1, 2025 (V1), the whole route minus 30 miles at $2.85 a mile. From Dec 1, 2025, loaded miles at $4.80. From Aug 18, 2026, loaded miles after the first 30.
  • Source: echo1 production database.
