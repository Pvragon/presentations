Rewrite the transport lifecycle help articles

Done · closed Oct 11HighWilliam TitusCreated Aug 28

The three articles about a ride's life in Echo1 disagree with each other and with the product. The fix is three new articles where every fact lives in exactly one place.

The problem and the fix

What's wrong today

  • The status guide uses old-system field names. Dispatchers go looking for statuses Echo1 doesn't have.
  • Cancellation billing is stated wrong in two places, in opposite directions.
  • Claim statuses are written three different ways.
  • One article says five stages, then lists six.
  • The same definitions sit in two articles, which is how they drifted.

Ground rules for the rewrite

  • Echo1 only. No old-system names, no TANF, no "Track 1–5".
  • Every payer is treated the same: a cancellation turns on whether the driver started travelling.
  • Outcomes and billing rules live only in Article 2. The other two link to it.
  • Don't document the undispatched outcome.

The idea the old articles miss

A transport has only three statuses. Completed, Cancelled and Voided are outcomes, recorded when the ride closes. Status says where the ride is now; outcome says what happened to it.

Two corrections that must land

  • Aborted is never billable. One old article says "TANF billable", another says "TANF exception applies". Both are wrong.
  • Withdrawn means "driver never left, with notice". The old text drops the notice part, which is what makes it non-billable.
