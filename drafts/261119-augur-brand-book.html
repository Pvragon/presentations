01 · Personality

Every copy has a place and a price.

How it looks

The collection's own rows are the picture. A headline is set in the mono face (font.family.display) and running text in font.family.body. The signature layout is a table of card, where it is, and price, read from the real collection and dated, with a one-line summary under a rule ("74 of the 100 cards owned, 26 to buy"). There are no illustrations and no card art in brand applications; card names carry the Magic content. Corners are small (radius.sm for controls, radius.md for panels). Motion is short and plain (motion.duration.press, motion.easing.ui); nothing bounces.

How colour is used

Augur looks like an inventory printout. The page is stone paper (color.palette.light.ground) with graphite ink (color.palette.light.ink), and panels sit one step darker on color.palette.light.surface. Secondary text uses color.palette.light.muted. The one colour is blueprint blue (color.palette.light.accent, white text on it from color.palette.light.accentInk): it marks the action on a view and the summary rule under a table, and nothing else. The dark theme keeps the same roles on color.palette.dark.ground, color.palette.dark.surface and color.palette.dark.ink, with the lighter blue color.palette.dark.accent. Rules between rows use color.palette.light.hairline and color.palette.dark.hairline.

04 · Logo

The collection held in the hand, every copy counted

Clear space and minimum size

The mark is a fan of four physical cards, drawn in one ink as solid rounded rectangles cut apart by one constant gap. It stands for the collection in the hand: every copy counted. Use it in one colour (ink on the light ground, light ink on the dark ground); it is never outlined, tilted further, recoloured per card or set inside a container shape. At small sizes use the dedicated small drawing, never a scaled-down master. Minimum size: the mark at 32 px, the lockup at 32 px tall. Below 32 px, such as a browser tab, use the small drawing, assets/mark-16.svg; the favicons are built from it. Clear space: a quarter of the mark's height on every side, kept free of type, edges and other marks. On a dark ground use the on-dark files; never recolour the mark in the brand colour on a busy picture.

Know before you buy it twice.

Pairsexact, not fussy; plain, not dull; dry, not cold; complete, not cluttered
Person"you" for the player; Augur as the subject of product sentences; "we" only in legal and support copy
Sentencesmostly 6 to 14 words; card names, set codes, places and counts written as they appear in the collection
