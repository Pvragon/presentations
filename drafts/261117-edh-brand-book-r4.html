01 · Personality

The reason sits beside the pick.

How it looks

The tool's own output is the design. A result reads like a printed land list: the card name, the reason in plain words, the price at the right edge, and a line under the list with the count behind it. The brand shows that list wherever another brand would show a picture, because the reason beside the pick is the thing edh.wtf does that others do not. Card names, counts and prices are set in a mono face (font.family.display), the way the tool prints them, so columns line up and a number never shifts. Running text is a plain, open sans (font.family.body). Headlines use the mono face at a medium weight; size carries them, not boldness. Corners are softly rounded (radius.sm for buttons, radius.md for panels). Motion is quick and plain (motion.duration.in, motion.easing.ui): a result arrives, it does not perform.

How colour is used

The page is a working surface. In the light theme, ink (color.palette.light.ink) sits on a cool off-white ground (color.palette.light.ground), and results sit on a slightly darker panel (color.palette.light.surface). One blue (color.palette.light.accent, color.palette.dark.accent in the dark theme) marks the one thing to act on: the button, the rule under the count, the pick being explained. It never fills a background. The dark theme (color.palette.dark.*) is the result screen at night: a blue-black ground with light ink, and the same blue lifted so it reads on the dark. Players check a build late, after a game, so the dark theme is designed, not inverted.

04 · Logo

The land you hold for your next land drop

Clear space and minimum size

A fanned hand of four cards with the fourth raised out of the hand: the land you hold for your next land drop. It is drawn from rounded rectangles with one constant gap, set in one colour, and sits beside the wordmark "edh.wtf" in the mono face. A separate small drawing, two cards, stands in for it at favicon size. Minimum size: the mark at 48 px, the lockup at 48 px tall. Below 48 px, such as a browser tab, use the small drawing, assets/mark-16.svg; the favicons are built from it. Clear space: a quarter of the mark's height on every side, kept free of type, edges and other marks. On a dark ground use the on-dark files; never recolour the mark in the brand colour on a busy picture.
