01 · Personality

Pull up a chair.

Positioning statement, internal: not public copy

ONE Mahjong is the only American mahjong card committed to opening its whole structure, every hand design and how hands are made, so no single organization controls the game's hands; its cards are free and new every season, and one set that plays every kind of mahjong is in the works.

How it looks

The card is the picture. Headlines are set in a sturdy slab (font.family.display) with plain, unambiguous digits, running text in font.family.body, and every hand line in the mono face (font.family.mono), whose marked zero keeps the Soap from reading as a letter. The signature layout is a section of the real card: the section title, its note from the card, and each hand on one line with its points aligned right. On the printed card each hand also carries its plain-words reading under it; the poster and screens show the hand and its points only. There are no tile photographs and no illustrations; the hands carry the mahjong content. The card a player prints at home is ink only on plain white paper, Letter or A4. The yellow field is never printed: a full flood streaks and drains a home printer, and the card must read the same from any printer in black. The lockup, the section, the hands and the independence notice keep their places; rules between hands print as thin grey lines. The chair leads at most three applications, as on the web page, the phone and the social square. On the poster and the card the hands lead and the chair is a small lockup. Corners are sharp (radius.sm for controls, radius.md for panels), because the first application is a printed card. Motion is short and plain (motion.duration.press, motion.easing.ui); nothing bounces.

How colour is used

ONE Mahjong looks like a sign on the clubhouse door: one flat field of lemon yellow (color.palette.light.ground) with near-black ink (color.palette.light.ink), and nothing else competing with it. Panels sit one step deeper on color.palette.light.surface, and secondary text uses color.palette.light.muted. The action colour in the light theme is the ink itself (color.palette.light.accent, with yellow text on it from color.palette.light.accentInk): a black button on a yellow field. The dark theme turns the field over. The ground is near-black (color.palette.dark.ground), panels sit on color.palette.dark.surface, text is a plain off-white (color.palette.dark.ink, secondary color.palette.dark.muted), and the yellow moves to the action (color.palette.dark.accent, with dark text from color.palette.dark.accentInk). Rules between rows use color.palette.light.hairline and color.palette.dark.hairline.

Open questions for the owner

  • The open card structure has no licence chosen yet; nothing may call it "open source" until one is.
  • The positioning statement's claim to be the only such card rests on one search, on 2026-11-21; check it again before that claim appears in public copy.
  • No trademark search has been run for "ONE Mahjong" (classes 9, 16, 28, 41); do one before any filing or paid launch.
  • The League's terms of use were not read; have counsel read them before the first card ships.
  • The chair is a plain seating pictogram: it reads clearly at its minimum size but is close to stock chair icons, so the name carries most of the identity.
  • "Pull up a chair." is used by many mahjong clubs: usable as a board line, never ownable as a tagline.
  • A registered design mark, Waterside Collections' PULL UP A CHAIR (reg. 5681755), is a front-view chair above those words, for online retail; ONE Mahjong pairs a front-view chair with the same line. The classes differ, but check before any merch or retail use.

04 · Logo

An empty chair: a seat at the table for anyone

Clear space and minimum size

The mark is a chair seen from the front, with a tall back, drawn in one ink by construction. It stands for a seat at the table for anyone: pull up a chair. Use it in one colour (ink on the yellow field, off-white on the dark ground); it is never outlined, tilted, recoloured in parts or set inside a container shape. At small sizes use the dedicated small drawing, never a scaled-down master. Minimum size: the mark at 32 px, the lockup at 32 px tall. Below 32 px, such as a browser tab, use the small drawing, assets/mark-16.svg; the favicons are built from it. Clear space: a quarter of the mark's height on every side, kept free of type, edges and other marks. On a dark ground use the on-dark files; never recolour the mark in the brand colour on a busy picture. As a cropped supergraphic, far above logo size, the mark takes the theme's action colour; the logo files stay one colour.

05 · Voice

The game belongs to the players.

Voiceself-aware and playful: the table in second person, present tense, with its literal facts (four copies, plain paper, the Charleston, the snack table)
Pairsplayful, never silly about the rules; warm, never sugary; open about how the card is made, never preachy about who owns the game; cheeky about the annual fee, never rude about the League
Person"you" and "your table" for the players; "we" for the people who build the card; the card is the subject in feature lines
Sentencesmostly 6 to 16 words; one longer sentence where the data needs explaining; numbers as digits with their unit
Humoura straight face about table life (jokers, snacks, the wall, the Charleston) with the fact right under the joke, in sub-lines, section notes and microcopy; none in the research numbers, the independence notice or anything about the League
NeverLeague names, initials or marks; "open source" or "published" about the structure before it ships; a buy button, a price or a ship date for the set before it is on sale; the incumbent's price; motive words (scam, racket, greedy, cartel, tax as an accusation); "largest ever"; the held gap figure; praise words about ourselves; "free" on the paid set; a joke aimed at a player or at the League
